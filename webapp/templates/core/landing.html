Features

How Guardian Agent Keeps Kids Safe

AI-powered protection that runs silently in the background — no disruption to gameplay, full peace of mind.

Voice Chat Monitoring

Listens to in-game voice chat and transcribes it in real time, detecting harmful language, grooming patterns, and threats.

Text Chat Analysis

Scans text messages across games and apps for inappropriate content, personal information sharing, and manipulative behavior.

AI-Powered Detection

Uses a fine-tuned LLM to understand context and intent, going far beyond simple keyword filters used by games.

Instant Parent Alerts

Get notified immediately when a risk is detected. View incident details and take action right from your phone.

Custom Safety Rules

Set age-appropriate protection levels and choose which games and apps to monitor. Full control in your hands.

Privacy-First Design

Runs locally on your computer. Audio and text are analyzed on-device — nothing is stored or shared with third parties.
